# Change of state

Every spec and every build, oldest first. For each: what it was, what was found wrong, what replaced it, where it lives now. The purpose is that a reader (a person or a model) can tell at a glance what is current and what is not, and can still read the old thing to see what went wrong.

Sources for every "found wrong" cell: the owner's words (date given; full text in `prose/`), or an audit in `audits/`. Nothing in this file is my own verdict on a build.
