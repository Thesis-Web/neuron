When the route does come (a define wants a word no source holds, or the brain is composing its own tools), the shape is the owner's **intent file router** (github Thesis-Web/exnulla-demos, apps/intent-file-router): a metadata header on line one names an allowlisted destination and a relative path, the payload is the body, gates refuse absolute paths, traversal, backslashes, denylisted extensions, oversize and binary; a sha256 is the route id; every step writes an audit event; the plan is shown, not blindly run. It is a browser planner, it does not itself execute. **4. The teaching principle, reaffirmed and saved.** Teaching is not telling. The model must NOT pre-compute the answer, author a whole formula, and bind it; that is memorize-and-execute, the v18 failure. When asked for lines, give questions and the simplest next step, and read back what the brain says in words (gate, shard, run, cell).
