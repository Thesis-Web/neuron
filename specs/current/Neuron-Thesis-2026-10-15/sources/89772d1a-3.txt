## WORKER ANALYSIS (Claude math, hypothesis, not owner text, not built):
1. It matches the owner's own existing-pattern rule: the brain ALREADY tracks "what was touched": receipts and memories carry times (dna.times, last_event), the sixth sense keeps dirty routes and an idle patrol, the clock runs bounded work. A sleep pass = the clock in a different mode over ONLY the touched trees (dirty set), never the corpus.
2. A tiny model per tree: its inputs are that tree's own local history (which arrival led to which continuation/answer, how often, with what outcome); its size scales with the tree's neighbourhood, not the corpus. That is the energy argument the owner makes (a full LLM lights up everything to guess).
3. Simplest first version (my proposal): counts before attention. Sleep computes, for each touched tree, how often each continuation followed each arrival and how often the answer held (matched the expected output). Today the "83 percent" in '6 double ?' is exactly such a weight, but host-computed in learn.py; moving its computation into a sleep run is the first honest step.
