Contract name: Neuron cognitive vocabulary v1 (NCV1). This is an assistant-authored working contract responding to the owner's terminology request, not a claim that natural-language words have universal meanings or that all cognition has a proven finite primitive basis. The owner has not separately ratified each definition. Scope: cognitive operations and their interfaces, not new L0 opcodes. Qualified identifier NCV1:WORD has the definition below. Bare English usage outside this contract is not changed.
Owner instruction: stop using the ambiguous umbrella term as a task or completion claim. A term must keep its value across LLM interpretations.
Contract rules:
- Use the exact reserved operation plus its operands. Do not silently substitute another operation.
- Each request specifies operation, target, inputs, relevant rule/criterion, budget, allowed state change, required evidence.
- If a required operand is not supplied by the task or retained authoritative context, keep it explicitly unresolved; do not guess a different task.
- Every operation may fail, abstain, or return unresolved status. Definitions describe the success contract, not guaranteed success.
- A stored record is not an adopted belief. A prediction is not an observation. A simulation is not an external observation. Replayed evidence is not independent evidence. A supplied rule is not an induced rule. An adopted model is not proven true.
- Preserve provenance, scope, assumptions, versions and uncertainty across operations.
- Test an operation's specific postcondition. Test producer-to-consumer delivery separately.
- Freeze v1 meanings; changes require an explicit new version or named amendment. Do not retroactively reinterpret old records.
- These contracts constrain the assistant's usage; no guarantee that every LLM follows them without being given the contract and checked against it.

Named sequences and measurable outcomes (not additional primitive aliases):
MEMORIZATION: STORE + INDEX + RETAIN; RECALL tests accessibility. No claim about new-case performance.
IMITATION: OBSERVE demonstrated behavior + INDUCE a candidate policy + TEST + FIT or REVISE + VALIDATE. COPY alone only reproduces content.
CALIBRATION: PREDICT + OBSERVE outcomes + SCORE using a calibration/proper-scoring criterion + FIT a probability mapping + VALIDATE. Merely displaying a percentage is insufficient.
REINFORCEMENT: EXECUTE or INTERVENE + OBSERVE outcome + SCORE under a declared reward rule + ATTRIBUTE credit + FIT or REVISE + subsequent TEST/VALIDATE. Reward is not an unspecified feeling of agreement.
EXPLORATION: INDUCE/ABDUCE candidate possibilities + RANK observation opportunities + SELECT + ASK or INTERVENE + OBSERVE. There must be an explicit information/objective criterion and budget.
CONSOLIDATION: a declared sequence chosen from RETAIN, MERGE, COMPRESS, SUMMARIZE, PRUNE and FORGET, followed by tests of retained behavior. It is not an unspecified sleep activity.
TRAINING: a declared repeated sequence of PREDICT/EXECUTE, OBSERVE, SCORE, FIT/REVISE and evaluation; the word alone is not an actionable instruction.
INFERENCE: choose DEDUCE, INDUCE, ABDUCE, ANALOGIZE, or PREDICT explicitly; do not interpret the broad label silently.
ADAPTATION: measured behavior change after specified FIT/REVISE and ADOPT steps in response to specified evidence; changing a counter alone proves only a counter change.
GENERALIZATION: demonstrated performance meeting a declared criterion on cases excluded from constructing/adjusting the method. INDUCE proposes a general rule; VALIDATE supplies bounded evidence for generalization.
TRANSFER: measured application of an existing method to a specified different task/context; success must be evaluated there. ANALOGIZE may propose a transfer, but does not prove it.
RETENTION (outcome): formerly demonstrated performance remains within declared tolerances after later changes.
IMPROVEMENT: a specified metric is better than a named baseline on a specified evaluation set, with declared coverage/cost constraints.
REGRESSION: the corresponding measured deterioration under comparable conditions.
AUTONOMY: the declared sequence is initiated and executed by the brain within the declared domain/budget without an LLM choosing each step, providing each answer, or patching each case. Observations from outside are allowed; scope and duration must be reported.
UNDERSTANDING, KNOWING, DISCOVERY, SELF-IMPROVEMENT and other umbrella success words cannot substitute for the named operations and evidence.
Supervised, unsupervised, self-supervised, online, continual and meta are protocol qualifiers, not primitive operations: specify where targets come from, when updates occur, which past performance must be retained, and whether the object being FIT/REVISEd is itself a selection/update procedure. They do not identify a complete process on their own.
