**The step-back, as steps.** [OWNER NEW; Q8 shows the same idea on 2026-09-23]
1. You have the current outcome O, the desired outcome D, and the receipt of the run that gave O.
2. Step back one node, N.
3. The candidates are ONLY the shards at N that arrived the same way as this run. Not every shard in N, never the corpus.
4. For each candidate, take its known outcome from its receipt and ask two questions:
   - Q1: does it equal D? If yes, finished.
   - Q2: is it closer to D than O?
5. If one is closer: it becomes the working answer. Step down into that run and repeat at its nodes.
6. If none is closer: step back to the node before N and repeat.

Three refinements:
- If nothing is closer, the honest record is "no KNOWN arrival-compatible shard here helps"; a shard not learned yet might. [B]
- Taking another shard changes the operation. A remainder of x4 from 3 gives 12, but that does not answer (1+2)+4, so the task must allow the change. [B]
