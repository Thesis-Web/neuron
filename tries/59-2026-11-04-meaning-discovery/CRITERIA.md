## Phase B, the selection method (candidates.py, candidates-hp.txt)
Over the test brain's own WordNet copy: every verb sense whose gloss (the text after `verb N:` up to the first bracketed field or quoted example) names two or more distinct grounded points; the headword a defined point with no acting row and no belief or tried row; printed in order of the count, then the word. 388 senses (275 counting only the pre-existing operations). The candidates are taken in the list's own order: the first four `ok` rows (catalogue, docket, plan, pulsate), then the first four `ok` rows of the two-word group (accommodate, adapt, affect, apologise). The negative control: abandon, the first `ok` headword in word order whose first sense names no grounded point. The context word (testc_find2.py): the first headword in word order with two verb senses whose glosses name different believed points, each sense with an example that contains the headword: assume (sense 3 form, sense 1 true).

## H1 (run 1, derive-run-1-hp.txt)
The operational meaning of X is the grounded points named in the gloss of its first WordNet verb sense, in gloss order, the first as the shard's call and the second as its spawn; grounded = a point with any dna row carrying a call or spawn; a point's run = its `:` call, else its first acting shard's call or spawn.
What run 1 showed: (1) `or`, `and`, `as`, `by`, `on` counted as grounded because the define cascade lays a spawn row at (define, :) on words of definitions; (2) my expected took WordNet's senses in corpus order while the brain takes them in node id order, so four expecteds named another sense than the brain's; (3) a test root (move) was picked for pulsate and failed when called outside its own grammar; (4) apologise gained call 19938846 (define's run) from its gloss word explain, and `apologise : who` ran define on who (the say holds who's definition cells); (5) abandon stayed unresolved; (6) a shard with no call appeared on abandon and accommodate: a keyed dna read by (gate, before, trigger) lays the shard when absent (confirmed on abdicate: a SELECT laid shard 20049858).

## H1a (run 2)
Grounded = a point with a shard the teacher confirmed (belief correct > 0, the brain's own record); its run = that shard's call (the most confirmed). The first verb sense = the first by node id. No dna read by (before, trigger) in the derivation (read by gate, filter in SQL).
