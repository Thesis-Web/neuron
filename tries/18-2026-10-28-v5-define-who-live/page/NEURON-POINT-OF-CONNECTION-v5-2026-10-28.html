How to read the stage

  • Grey points are the 80 hand-laid neurons (the symbols of the born run, the arrivals, the sources' gates, the cell names). Blue points were born by the born run. Bright purple holds definition rows. Orange has a shard with a call or spawn (a formula bound to it).
  • A run is drawn through the gates of its receipt in order; stepping lights the hop and flashes what the shard called.
  • Traversals are read from the receipts: every pair of gates hit one after the other (the space skipped), brighter the more often; the layout's springs follow them, so what the brain used together sits together. The grey DNA lines are the rows themselves (before → gate → trigger); most run through the space neuron, which is why they are off unless you switch the space on.
  • The stage is the brains now. Every read is a SELECT through the door; nothing is replayed from a log.

What is read, from where

  • Points: main.label. Lines: every DNA table ("k"), the rel table, the call/spawn columns, and the receipts (run_receipt, consecutive gates per run) for the traversals.
  • Step: run_receipt. Run: run_sheet (the cells), run_memory, timeline, out_mother, secondary, the child runs.
  • Selected: the DNA table of the gate, its definition node ("k.node"), the memory rows on its shards, the rel rows.
  • Brains: sqlite_master counts of main and the sixth, the sixth's inputs, guess.guesses, the refused table.

Type a line

Start over lays both eggs and the source brains afresh (the door runs the same sqlite3 and make_sources.py as the terminal); the 48 lines are LINES-v5.txt typed in order, about two minutes on a Linux path.

The user's prompt writes into main.input; the sixth's into sixth.input (they never share). BUILD … BUILD hands the symbols between to mother. A symbol with no gate is born first. A new word that the sources hold takes a few seconds (the births of every symbol in its definitions).
