## What the brain does on its own, and what is told

**On its own (physics, hand laid once, every step a row the brain reads):** cut a typed line into symbols and give a new symbol the next gate; land on each gate in turn; write the shard if the arrival and the next are new, count it if not; write the receipt row; when the next gate has no table, write the newborn's number on the run's sheet and excite born inline before landing; replay a run that a shard's call or spawn column names; decode the symbols between two BUILD gates, fill the holes from the sheet (own run, then the run above, then the one above that), hand the text to mother with the key (run, cell, who, when); take her answer and every source's answer as a return run through the encoder, and when it ends fill the cell it was asked for and land a row in the definition node of the symbol it was about; write the memory on the shard that ended the run with the sheet inside, the timeline row, and stop; tell the sixth which run ended; when no declared cell is empty, put the sheet in the out mailbox.

**Told (typed, the brain lays the rows):** the born run; the three standing questions; the ask; the define run (what, the ask, the relationships); the sixth's memory of its asks and its observing line; the why line; correct and incorrect; and every bind, which is the brain updating its own DNA row through its prompt. The two words the brains pass each other, `seen` (physics, main to the sixth) and `why` (the sixth's line, to main), are mine; he may rename them.

**Never:** a timer; a sentence laid for the brain to say (the out mailbox holds the sheet's numbers and the returns' lines; the bottom and the page only read them back); a word in the graph (the encoder holds the spellings); the user's prompt used by the sixth or by mother.

## The guessing brain, labelled

`sources/guess.db` is the simplest guesser, not the hand-built llm the owner described: asked about a word, it answers the word that occurs most with it in the corpus the main brain pushed to it (the words of the sources' returns), with confidence = the share of corpus lines holding the word that hold the guess, and belief = at most 0.5 on first use; the user's correct and incorrect lines move the belief by 0.25 (my number). With nothing in its corpus for the word it answers nothing. Each ask is remembered with the count of source returns main held, so the sixth never asks twice on the same knowledge.

## Measured on the way, so it is not forgotten

A hop nested inside the hop before it costs more the deeper it is (0.7 ms at depth 50, 4.5 ms at depth 800, SQLite keeping every enclosing statement's cursors open), and a trigger per character of the typed line cost the same way; so the brain writes each next step into the hop neuron's table and mother pastes them flat, and the encoder cuts a line in one recursive query. Prepared-text memory in mother on its own changed nothing. On the Windows drive through drvfs a line is ten times slower than on ext4. Text exec'd by mother is at the one prompt: an unqualified name means main, so the sixth's texts say `sixth.`.
