What I changed, and why

  • Opposite flips a sign along one axis instead of reflecting through the center of the brain. In your map cold sits on the far side of everything from hot. Here hot and cold share the feel region and sit on opposite sides of it, up and down the polarity axis. Your own pseudocode said this: on is y, off is minus y. One short hop, and a run that passes both stays in one place.
  • Is a kind is the radial axis. Your map has 11,987 kind lines and places nothing with them. Here a broader word sits one shell inward, a narrower one one shell outward.
  • The center is the physical actions, then the primes, then words. In your map the roots of the sphere are whichever words the trials happened to leave unplaced: hot, up, die, no. Here a root is read, write, compare, move, clock. A word's direction is set by what it is made of, and its relationships adjust it from there.
  • Tense, number and affixes are a small fixed step on a form axis, not a new place. walked stays beside walk.
  • Your map's kind is the gentle one, opposite(cruel), and memory is opposite(forgetfulness). Two meanings on one gate, or a meaning placed only by its opposite. The map is already showing what is wrong. That is what it is for.

Every word to prime and prime to action formula on this page is my guess, made so the picture has something to stand on. The relationships and polarities are your brain's own lines. The runs are the model from the last page.
