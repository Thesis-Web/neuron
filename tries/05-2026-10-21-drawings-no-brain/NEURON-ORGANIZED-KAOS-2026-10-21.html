How to read it

  • A dot is a gate, a word's table. Dots cluster by what the words are for: SQL words, memory and time, questions, sources, actions, language. The white dots in the middle are the told gates.
  • A line is a run. Its path through the dots is its shape. Its color is its kind. Deeper runs are fainter.
  • Every run is made of the same thing as the main run: hops from gate to gate on shards. A shard with a trigger pointer is where a new line starts.
  • The two far dots are the only places a run leaves the rows: sources, outside the brain, and mother, which runs SQL text.
  • Every run ends at the end gate. The main run goes on through makes sense, memory, timeline and say to the one output.
  • Time is the order rows land. Inside SQLite one typed line runs as one nested chain, in order, and finishes before the next line. The beat is what lets several lines run side by side.

The runs here are a model with the owner's names for the kinds and plausible long forms. The real long forms are the told lines, and the real counts are whatever the brain's rows say.
