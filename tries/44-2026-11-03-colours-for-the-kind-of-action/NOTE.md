## What a hop is, for the colour

A run's receipt walks symbols. For a typed line the first hops are the line's own symbols; between two BUILD gates the symbols spell the
statement mother is handed. A called or spawned run's whole text is one statement (`BUILD INSERT OR REPLACE INTO run_sheet ...`). So the
kind of action at a hop is the kind of the statement its hop belongs to, read from the symbols: the write target after INTO, UPDATE or
DELETE FROM names it (run_memory → writing a memory; sixth.third or told → asking the sixth; run_sheet, clock, timeline → state and time;
formula, pull, xyz, dna or a gate's own numbered table → binding a formula; rel, belief, tried, relmap, catalog → applying a meaning;
label, newgate, born → a birth; any other table → another write); a statement with no target that reads is reading a source; the
line's own symbols are the line itself; a branch run with no statement read stays orange.
