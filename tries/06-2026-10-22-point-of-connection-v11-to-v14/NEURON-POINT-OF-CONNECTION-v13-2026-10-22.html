Hand laid, exactly

  • 14 physical actions: READ, WRITE, COMPARE, ADD, SUBTRACT, MOVE, FIND, SPLIT, JOIN, CLOCK, HEAR, SAY, CALL, SPAWN. These are the silicon. Nothing in the brain can make them.
  • 16 physics tables, each with its trigger: input, encoder, decide, lay, born, source, agree, end, sense, memory, timeline, say, BUILD, formula, var, state. A trigger cannot CREATE, so the first of these cannot be born; they are laid once, as one block.
  • mother: the one function loaded beside SQLite. In your words, she does no work other than the traverse: the brain cannot change her, so she does only what the brain cannot do, which is leave its database and come back. She carries the brain's output to the SQL prompt or to another database's mailbox, and when that mailbox is populated she carries the reply back in as an input of the run that asked, into the cell the brain opened for it. Not a timer: populated. The brain decides what goes where; mother only routes.
  • the sources: databases outside the brain, brain-like in structure, each with a mailbox: WordNet, Webster 1913, Merriam-Webster and its thesaurus, Moby, your relation map, and the SQLite docs for the SQL words. In the real stack they live on hp and hp2 and can call out themselves; this page cannot, so it carries a slice of them fetched on 2026-10-22.
  • The primes are born by typing like any word, on their own shell. What each one does is not told. The first version carried a guessed table that said, for example, that people is FIND and ADD. You caught it: that does not balance what the symbol means, and it was an assignment. It is gone. A prime has no action until a test gives it one.

Everything else on the stage is born by typing: every word gate, every shard, every hop trigger, every relation, every meaning, every process. A process arrives taught, by one typed line; after three memories of the same receipt it is learned and has a shortcut; after that it is a hop. That is the arc from told to automated, and the point of connection is when nothing is left on the absent list.

The processes are the words

  • There is no process called qlike. There is the word like. When a run passes it, its formula fires: like = write :head relation. A definition row "like shop" runs with store as its subject, passes like, and like writes store alike shop. opposite and kind work the same way. derive and deduce are words too: born fires them on a new word, and typing "derive the opposite of below" fires derive on its object.
  • A word's meaning is its formula. word = ... is the only told form. Primes only: the word runs those actions when passed. Holes and primitive runs: a formula. Words starting with a colon are holes filled from the run that passes the word: :head, :arrival, :rest, and :subject, :verb, :object cut at the first being word. find, read, compare, join and write are SQL lines: the brain runs them through its gates as a branch (SELECT, value, FROM, state, WHERE, key, =), mother runs the decoded text, and the answer returns to input as a sub-input of the run, filling a cell. split, move and say are physical actions over the words after them. A formula may name another word with a formula, and it fires inside.
  • The trigger is a column on the DNA row. By default a row fires the word's own formula. what are -> who lays, or updates, the row in what for remainder are so it fires who instead. who is that -> about does it in the being verb itself, keyed by what came before and what comes after. So who is one neuron with many rows, and the remainder picks the row, and the row picks what runs, where it looks, what it says.
  • Learning is the same for all of them. A word's formula is taught by one line, learned after three memories of the same receipt, automated after that. Click any orange dot and read its formula and its rows.

Two parts to a meaning, and nothing assigned

  • What it is made of is a line: the word on the left, words on the right. What it does is the descent: the run that passes the word fires its row, the row fires the words behind it, and so on down until physical actions fire. The card shows both, with the depth and the words that have nothing behind them yet.
  • No line on this page is a meaning I picked. The shop lines are gone; they were me assigning. What stays is mechanism: the physics, the primes as words, the operators as words, and a few lines that say what a mechanism word does to the rows (like writes a relation; who looks in state). Those are labeled as mine and are to be replaced by what tests show.
  • Operators are words, from your roots worksheet: minus, plus, a lot more, twice, the inverse. In a formula, minus flips the sign of the next word, slash is a part and adds SPLIT, star adds JOIN, plus adds ADD. below = - above is your example: below does what above does with the sign flipped. divide and slash as two spellings of one gate is the encoder's job.
  • Hops carry operators too. A hop is a plus by default: continue. Typed between two words, - steps the run back one gate, so the next word is hit from the earlier arrival and a different row fires. * forks a test run with the same remainder, arriving as test, and compares its receipt with the main run's at the end. / is a choice: the path with an existing row is taken, otherwise the path is taken as a guess and the output says so. ** and sqrt have no mechanics yet and say so. These are my proposals for what an operator hop does to the run, and nothing about what any word means.
  • Heard is not said. A typed line's first row arrives from heard. "Say the last answer back" feeds the brain's own words to its ear, arriving from said: a different first row in the same neuron, and I and you swap when a formula looks in state.

The meaning formula, as this page measures it

  • What a word does is measured, not assigned. The line runs again with the word and again without it, on an exact replay of the brain up to that line, and the difference is read in every ledger: the fourteen physical actions, the graph (gates born, DNA rows, hop triggers, relations, tables, table rows, rows said), memory, state and timeline rows, the questions it fired, and the output. That difference is the word's meaning in that line. The shares between silicon, graph and questions are the ratios you asked about.
  • Modifiers are the other lines. The same word measured in each line it appeared in gives a record per context. who in "who are you ?" and who in "who is that ?" are two records, and the difference between them is what the other words did to who.
  • Composition is the descent. A word with a formula shows what it is made of and how many levels down it reaches the silicon. The measured difference and the descent are the two parts of its meaning, and they must agree, or the formula is wrong.
  • Roots are measured from mother. mother now keeps real tables and rows. Measured: CREATE makes a table exist; INSERT adds a row; SELECT reads rows and says them back and changes nothing; DELETE removes rows. Take CREATE out of its line and mother refuses the line. That is what the root means, and nobody typed it.
  • What this cannot do: say what store means. A word with no runs behind it measures as nothing but its own birth, and the page says so. Giving store runs that are right all the way down is the unsolved part, and this page refuses to fake it.
