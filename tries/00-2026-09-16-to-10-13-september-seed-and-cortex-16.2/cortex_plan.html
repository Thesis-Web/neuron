The loop, one turn

1. Teacher writes a hearing row (SAID, KIND, APPLY) in front of the student.
2. Structure executes APPLY; neurons change; structure writes sight / touch / smell / taste / internal rows with WHO = structure.
3. Observer receives the change-set (every cell written this turn) and nothing else.
4. Student may write its own hearing rows (me do), form conjectures, install a belief.
5. Student emits at its voice level: a signal, a pointer, an echo, an answer, an explanation, a sentence.
6. Teacher grades on taste (RIGHT/WRONG), asks who / what / when / where / how / why / with what / to what effect, or re-teaches.
