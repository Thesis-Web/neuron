# The geometry on the brain's own rows, 2026-10-30 19:30 to 20:20 (geo1, geo2, geo3, geo3b)

The owner's method: every kind of line has a law (a pull to a rest distance, a push across the origin, a step in a direction); the layout settles under all the laws at once; stand on a word whose alike, opposite and more-general words are known, plot from them where it should live, measure the miss, adjust the pulls and pushes, settle again. Done outside the brain in numpy (the hub), on the brain's own rows pulled from the kernel on hp (hp-pullrel.py): 229,760 relationship rows (like and opposite) and 315,831 pointer rows read from the texts of its WordNet and Wiktionary definition rows (hypernym, hyponym, synonyms, antonyms, derived, related, holonym ...), which the brain holds but has not laid as relationship rows (that rule is the owner's).

## geo3: fixed laws, settled to rest with momentum, on the core (40,805 points with two or more lines)

## geo3b: the pull of a line weighted by how exclusively its two words keep company (1/sqrt(deg·deg)), unrelated points pushed apart under 1.5
The cloud took a shape (extents 1.55, 6.13, 10.33). Signatures appeared: like pairs 0.91 apart where random pairs sit 1.86; opposite pairs across the origin (cosine −0.50); a word and its more-general word 0.44 apart and on the same side (cosine +0.82); derived forms 1.97 apart. A word is held where its many like lines hold it; one to three general or opposite lines do not single it out among forty thousand.

## What this says, as I read it
- The owner's point held: with equal pulls the graph is a ball; with the pull weighted by exclusivity and a push between the unrelated, the kinds of line get geometric signatures on real data, in three dimensions, in one evening.
- Locating a point from a few lines needs lines that are one-to-one or few-to-one (opposite nearly is; more-general is many-to-one). The like lines carry the identity, and they are the ones a plot cannot use without already standing on the word.
- The laws' strengths are mine (4/sqrt(deg·deg), rest 1, push under 1.5, across 0.3). The owner's words name what should set them: how often a line was laid, belief, time, state. None of those are in these rows yet; the rel row has a run but not a count.
- Everything here is a measurement beside the brain. To become the brain's, the kinds of line must be laid as relationship rows by its own define rule (the owner's call), and the laws must become formulas it can run.
